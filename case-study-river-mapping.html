Project Summary & Flight Objective

North Florida river community members commissioned an aerial inspection operation to investigate localized water clarity degradation. The explicit flight goal was to gather high-resolution, unedited aerial imagery to trace mixing plumes back to their exact source infrastructure origin points.

Evidentiary Flight Blueprint: Missions were planned utilizing specific altitude parameters to maximize visibility of the underwater boundary line, documenting water density changes across different channels.

Aviation Execution & Mapping Discoveries

Flight data clearly captured a clean baseline upstream of a distinct drainage canal intersection. The aerial captures isolated dark, opaque effluent spilling straight from the canal, creating an obvious boundary layer that tracked flowing downstream for over a quarter of a mile.

Community Advocacy Impact

The unedited geospatial media and data logs served as core visual evidence during an official community council environment meeting. This imagery provided community stakeholders with clear, undeniable evidence to map out structural remediation workflows.
